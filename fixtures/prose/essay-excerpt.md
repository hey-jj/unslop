# On the river path

The council opened the river path in 1974, and it has been walked every day
since. That is the whole claim, and it set the stage for a longer argument
about who the town builds for.

Walk it on a Tuesday and you will pass the same four people. The path covers
everything from commuting to grieving, which is a strange sentence to write
about four hundred metres of gravel. It was widened once, in 1998, after a
petition that nobody remembers signing, demonstrating the town's long habit
of getting there eventually.

I walked it twice last week to check the surface. The gravel is loose at the
north end and the drain by the bench is blocked again.

The future looks bright.
